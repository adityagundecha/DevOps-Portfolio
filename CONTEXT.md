# DevOps Portfolio

The personal portfolio site for Aditya Gundecha, a Lead Senior DevOps Engineer. It presents career history and flagship work to prospective employers, clients, and collaborators.

## Language

**Engineering Leader**:
Aditya's positioning identity — a platform/DevOps leader who sets technical direction, delivery strategy and engineering culture, and grows the teams that deliver. The site leads with "Engineering Leader — Platform & DevOps" rather than an unheld title (e.g. Head of / Director), targeting Engineering Manager, Head of Platform and Director of Engineering roles.
_Avoid_: DevOps Engineer, individual contributor framing (when describing the overall positioning)

**Platform as a Product**:
The guiding philosophy that the internal platform is treated like any product — with users, a roadmap and outcomes — so teams build on it rather than fight it.
_Avoid_: Platform team, internal tooling (as a philosophy label)

**How I Lead**:
The site section stating leadership philosophy as a short set of principles (platform-as-product, mentoring-as-multiplier, shift-left, autonomy-with-guardrails). Distinct from the career timeline.
_Avoid_: Leadership style, values

**Leadership Impact**:
The band of cross-cutting, people-and-outcome proof points (team size led, engineers mentored, savings owned, transformation led) that evidence leadership scale independent of any one role.
_Avoid_: Stats, achievements (as the section name)

**Experience**:
A role held at a company over a date range, carrying a title and a set of achievements. Forms the career timeline.
_Avoid_: Job, position, work history entry

**Project**:
A curated, named initiative reframed as an outcome-driven case study — the problem, what was built, the technology used, and the measurable result. Distinct from an Experience: a Project is a single piece of work, often lifted out of a role, chosen to showcase impact.
_Avoid_: Case study (in UI copy), portfolio item, work

**Achievement**:
A single accomplishment within an Experience, stated as an outcome. The bullet-level unit that makes up a role.
_Avoid_: Responsibility, duty, task

**Competency**:
A high-level capability area (e.g. Agile Transformation, Cloud Architecture) summarising what Aditya does, independent of any one role.
_Avoid_: Skill (reserve "Skill" for concrete tools/technologies), strength
